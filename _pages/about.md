## About

I am an AI research and innovation leader specialising in the development and translation of artificial intelligence technologies for speech, language and digital health.

As **Senior Director of Speech Analytics at Redenlab**, I lead research and innovation across speech AI, machine learning and digital biomarker development, with a particular focus on neurological and neurodevelopmental disorders. My work spans the full research lifecycle — from identifying clinically meaningful problems and developing new computational methods, through scientific validation, to translation into scalable clinical research technologies. I lead the development of digital biomarkers used in clinical trials across more than **300 sites in 25 countries**, including the speech data pipelines, remote monitoring workflows and speech-based endpoint analysis tools that support cross-site, multilingual studies.

I lead multidisciplinary research programs involving speech foundation models, machine learning, automatic speech recognition, acoustic and phonetic modelling, natural language processing, digital biomarkers and generative AI. A major focus of my work is developing objective, AI-derived endpoints that can quantify clinically meaningful change in speech and communication.

My role also involves establishing research strategy, leading scientific collaborations, developing grant-funded research programs, mentoring scientists, and working with clinical, academic and industry partners to translate emerging AI research into robust and clinically meaningful capabilities.

Alongside my industry role, I am a **Senior Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Melbourne**, Department of Audiology and Speech Pathology, where I contribute to research at the intersection of artificial intelligence, speech science and clinical research and supervise graduate students. Clinically, my work focuses on speech and language analysis in neurological populations, including ataxia, Friedreich's ataxia, ALS, multiple sclerosis and dementia.

## Research & Innovation

**Clinical AI & Digital Biomarkers**
Developing and validating AI-derived speech and language measures as objective digital biomarkers and clinical endpoints for neurological and neurodevelopmental disorders.

**Speech Foundation Models & Machine Learning**
Investigating self-supervised learning, transformer architectures and speech foundation models for extracting clinically meaningful representations from speech, including measures of articulation, intelligibility, naturalness, phonation, prosody and timing.

**AI-Based Speech & Phonetic Analytics**
Developing automated approaches for syllable segmentation, phoneme detection and alignment, pathological voice activity detection, speaker diarization, acoustic event detection and other components required for scalable clinical speech analysis.

**Natural Language & Generative AI**
Developing machine learning and NLP methods for the quantitative analysis of clinical language, including automated linguistic biomarkers, multilingual language analysis, large language models and emerging generative AI approaches.

**Responsible & Reproducible Clinical AI**
Establishing rigorous approaches to model validation, reproducibility, dataset and model provenance, performance monitoring and scientific governance, to support the responsible translation of AI models into clinical and academic research.

## Selected Research Programs

**Automated Research Audio Analysis**
Leading the development of an AI-driven research audio analysis and quality assurance framework incorporating automated task boundary detection, acoustic event detection, speaker counting and diarization, speaker-role identification, noise detection and pathological-speech voice activity detection.

**AI-Derived Clinical Endpoints**
Leading the development and validation of machine-learning-derived speech endpoints across multiple neurological disease programs, integrating acoustic, phonetic, prosodic and learned speech representations with clinically meaningful outcomes.

**Speech Intelligibility & Naturalness**
Developing machine learning approaches that transform perceptual assessments of speech intelligibility and naturalness into scalable, reproducible quantitative measures for clinical research.

**Phonetic & Temporal Speech Modelling**
Developing AI methods for syllable segmentation, phoneme detection and alignment, speech timing, and emerging measures of stress and articulatory behaviour in typical and pathological speech.

**Blood2Voice** *(exploratory research / work in progress)*
An exploratory research program investigating whether information embedded in speech can contribute to the prediction of molecular biomarkers associated with neurological disease, bridging digital phenotyping with molecular measures.

**Clinical ASR Validation**
Investigating the clinical validity of automatic speech recognition systems beyond conventional word error rate, including how transcription errors propagate into downstream linguistic biomarkers, diagnostic models and clinical AI outcomes.

## Career Path

Signal Processing & Engineering → Speech Science & Phonetics → Machine Learning & AI → Clinical Speech AI → Digital Biomarkers → AI Research & Innovation Leadership

I completed my **Ph.D. *summa cum laude*** in Computational Linguistics / Phonetics and Speech Sciences at the **University of Zurich** (2015–2019), funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) and a Forschungskredit Candoc Scholarship. Prior to that I held an ERASMUS MUNDUS fellowship, earning M.Sc. degrees from **KTH Royal Institute of Technology** (Stockholm) and **Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya** (Barcelona), following a B.Eng. in Electronics and Communications Engineering from Anna University, Chennai.

My professional experience spans clinical-trial speech technology (Redenlab), ASR research (Telepathy Labs, Zurich), COVID-19 vocal biomarker diagnostics (VoiceMed, Luxembourg), forensic speaker verification (Centre for Forensic Phonetics and Acoustics), and enterprise IT (Tata Consultancy Services).

I speak regularly on deepfake voice technology and its implications for security and society — most recently as an invited speaker at the Identity and Access Management Summits in Melbourne (2025) and Sydney (2026). I have also been an invited keynote speaker at the International Virtual Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Statistics (IVCAIS 2021), an invited expert in an international technology-transfer programme between the University of Zurich, Sweden and the National Institute of Informatics (NII), Japan, and a guest on Swiss national television (SRF *Einstein*) and radio explaining deepfake voice technology to a general audience.
