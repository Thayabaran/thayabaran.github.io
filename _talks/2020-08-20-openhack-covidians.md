Panel discussion in Openhack's *COVIDians & Their Solutions* series, on VoiceMed's approach to rapid COVID-19 screening: an AI system combining phone-recorded voice and cough data with clinical survey information to return a result in around two minutes.

Appearing as Speech Scientist at VoiceMed and postdoctoral researcher at the University of Zurich, alongside Jonė Pukėnaitė (Medical Affairs Manager, VoiceMed) and Cedric Tamavond (Product Lead, VoiceMed), moderated by Inês Boski. The panel covered VoiceMed's development from hackathon origins through to clinical validation, and the role of AI in healthcare more broadly.
