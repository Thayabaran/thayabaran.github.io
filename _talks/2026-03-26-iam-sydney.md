Invited "How I Solved…" session on defending against deepfake voice threats. As synthetic audio becomes more realistic and easier to generate, voice authentication systems are being put to the test. Drawing on research and implementation experience across speech science, healthcare and AI, the talk covered real-world cases where deepfake voice was used or detected, and the lessons they hold for identity and access management teams:

- **Where deepfake voice attacks are already landing** — real examples of compromise attempts and what they expose about current system weaknesses.
- **How to build detection and resilience into voice authentication** — techniques to identify synthetic speech, build layered defences and flag anomalies early.
- **What security teams need to prepare for next** — the future of synthetic voice, risks to high-stakes workflows, and how to stay ahead of attacker innovation.
