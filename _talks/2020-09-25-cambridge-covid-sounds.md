Invited speaker at the First Virtual Workshop on Automatic Diagnostics of COVID-19 Sounds, organised by Prof. Cecilia Mascolo (Department of Computer Science and Technology) and Prof. Pietro Cicuta (Department of Physics), University of Cambridge, and sponsored by the European Research Council through Project EAR.

Presented work from VoiceMed on extracting clinically meaningful information from the human voice using machine learning, including the design of a COVID-19 screening tool built on vocal and cough biomarkers.

Other invited speakers included Rita Singh (Carnegie Mellon University), Björn Schuller (Imperial College London), Vered Aharonson (Afeka College of Engineering) and Lara Orlandic (EPFL).
