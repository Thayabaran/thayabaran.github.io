Book chapter (open access, CC BY-SA 4.0) from the University of Zurich Research Priority Program "Language and Space". *Shared first authorship.*

We present SEE-U-VR (Speech Environment Elicitation Using Virtual Reality), a virtual reality tool for eliciting infant-directed, pet-directed, public and read speech, which can be extended to other speaking styles. In a speech production study with five female speakers, infant- and pet-directed speech elicited in VR showed higher f0 mean, wider f0 range and longer vowel durations than read speech, mirroring laboratory findings, while VR-elicited public speech did not differ from read speech. SEE-U-VR offers a more natural yet controlled way to collect different speaking styles, with potential uses in areas such as dialect research in multilingual Switzerland.
